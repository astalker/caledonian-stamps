# Location of Meetings of Glasgow Caledonian Philatelic Society

Meetings of the Glasgow Caledonian Philatelic Society will now take place in the new location at

**Suite E/F,**

**8 Victoria Chambers,**

**142 West Nile Street,**

**G1 2RD**

The room is on the **first floor** and there is a lift from the entrance. You will need to use the buzzer to gain access to the building when you arrive.

The Glasgow Stamp Shop's new premises are on the same floor at the top of the stairs at the front of the building. It is a good opportunity to give them extra custom.

Afternoon meetings will still be at 1.30pm but the evening meetings will be held at 7pm
